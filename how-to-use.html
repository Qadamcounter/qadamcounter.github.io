How to Use Qadam

1. Install the app

  1. Download the app file from our home page (or install Qadam from the Amazon Appstore).
  2. Open the file. If your phone asks "Allow from this source", tap Allow — this is normal when installing an app from outside the Play Store.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

2. Allow the step permission

When Qadam opens for the first time, it will ask for the Physical activity permission. Tap Allow. This permission lets the app read your phone's step sensor. Without it, the app cannot count your steps. Qadam does not ask for your location, contacts or photos — it does not need them.

3. Just walk

Keep your phone in your pocket or bag and walk as you normally do. The big number on the home screen is your step count for today. Around it, the goal ring fills up as you move toward your daily goal (10,000 steps by default). Under the ring you can see your distance, calories burned and walking time.

Tip: for the most accurate counting, open Qadam once in the morning so it is running, and carry the phone on your body — steps cannot be counted if the phone is lying on a table.

4. Listen for the voice

Every time you complete another 100 steps, a friendly voice will cheer you on with a short motivational message. Bigger celebrations come at 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 steps. Keep your phone's volume up if you want to hear them — many walkers tell us this little voice is what keeps them going on the last round of the park.

5. Check your week

Open the History screen to see your last 7 days as a simple chart. Try to beat yesterday, or at least match it. On the Badges screen you can see the badges you have earned at 5,000, 10,000 and 15,000 steps in a day, and your current daily streak.

6. Set your own goal

10,000 steps is a good target, but it is not a rule. If you are just starting, set a smaller goal — 5,000 or 6,000 steps — and increase it slowly as walking becomes a habit. A goal you can reach most days is better than a big goal that discourages you. Read our 12 tips to reach 10,000 steps a day when you are ready to level up.

Troubleshooting

  • Steps are not counting: check that the Physical activity permission is allowed (Phone Settings → Apps → Qadam → Permissions), and open the app once after restarting your phone.
  • Counting stops when the screen is off: some phones aggressively close background apps. In your phone's battery settings, set Qadam to "Unrestricted" or "Don't optimise".
  • The voice is silent: check the phone is not on silent/vibrate mode and the media volume is up.
  • Counts look too high or low: step sensors count movement, so travelling in a vehicle on a rough road can add a few steps. Carrying the phone in a pocket gives the best accuracy.
