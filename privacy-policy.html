Privacy Policy for Qadam: Step Counter & Walker

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Qadam ("the App") is a step counter that helps you track your daily walking activity.

Data we collect

  • Step and activity data: The App counts your steps using your device's built-in step sensor. This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Settings you enter (such as weight, step goal and stride length) are stored only on your device.
  • Advertising: The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use an advertising identifier and related data to serve ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Permissions

  • Physical activity / activity recognition: required to count your steps.
  • Notifications: used only for optional daily goal reminders, which you can turn off in Settings.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Step data never leaves your device except through the advertising services described above.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
