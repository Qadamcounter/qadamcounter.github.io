About Qadam

Qadam: Step Counter & Walker is a free pedometer app for Android. "Qadam" (قدم) is an Urdu word that simply means "a step" — and that is the whole idea of the app: your health improves one step at a time, and Qadam counts every one of them for you.

Why we built Qadam

Most step counter apps are heavy, full of complicated screens, locked features and sign-up forms. Many of them stop working properly without internet, and most are only in English. We wanted the opposite: a light, honest app that anyone can use — including people in Pakistan and South Asia who are more comfortable in Urdu, people with budget phones, and people who just want to press one button and walk.

Qadam works completely offline. It uses your phone's built-in step sensor, so it does not need GPS, does not drain your battery tracking your location, and does not need an account. Your step data stays on your own phone.

What makes Qadam different

  • A voice that walks with you: after every 100 steps, a friendly voice cheers you on with motivational words, and celebrates with you at 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 steps.
  • Urdu + English: the app speaks and displays in a mix that feels natural for our users.
  • A clear daily goal: a big goal ring (10,000 steps by default) shows your progress at a glance.
  • History and badges: your last 7 days are saved, and you earn badges and streaks as you hit milestones.
  • Light and free: a small download, no sign-up, no subscription, no locked features.

Who is Qadam for?

Qadam is for anyone who wants to move more: people trying to lose weight, people managing blood sugar or blood pressure on their doctor's advice, office workers who sit all day, students, homemakers, and older adults who enjoy a daily walk. If you can walk, Qadam can help you walk a little more every day.

Our promise

We keep Qadam simple and honest. The app shows ads to stay free, but it never sells your personal data, and your walking history never leaves your phone. If you have an idea to make Qadam better, we genuinely want to hear it — visit our contact page.
