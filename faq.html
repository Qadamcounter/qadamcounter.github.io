Frequently Asked Questions

Is Qadam really free?

Yes. Qadam is completely free to download and use. All features — step counting, distance, calories, history, badges and the voice motivation — are free. The app shows ads, which is how we keep it free for everyone.

Does Qadam work without internet?

Yes. Step counting works fully offline because it uses your phone's built-in step sensor. You only need internet if you want to open links or load ads — your walking is never dependent on a connection.

How accurate is the step counting?

Qadam reads the same hardware step sensor that expensive fitness apps use, so accuracy is generally very good when the phone is carried in a pocket or in your hand. No phone sensor is perfect: very soft movements may be missed, and bumpy vehicle rides can occasionally add a few steps.

Does Qadam drain my battery?

No. The step sensor is one of the lowest-power sensors in your phone, and Qadam does not use GPS at all. In normal use its battery effect is very small. If counting ever stops in the background, set Qadam to "Unrestricted" in your phone's battery settings.

Why does Qadam ask for the Physical activity permission?

Android requires this permission before any app can read the step sensor. It is the only sensitive permission Qadam needs. Qadam does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or photos.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Your steps and history are stored locally in the app and are not uploaded to us. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

What is the voice that speaks every 100 steps?

It is Qadam's motivational voice. Every 100 steps it encourages you with a short message, with special celebrations at 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 steps. Make sure your media volume is turned up to hear it.

How many steps should I walk every day?

10,000 steps a day is a popular goal, but research shows real benefits start much lower — even 6,000–8,000 steps a day is far better than 2,000. Start where you are, add about 500 steps a week, and build up slowly. Our walking tips page shows how.

The app stopped counting. What should I do?

First, open Qadam once — after a phone restart the app needs to be opened again. Then check the Physical activity permission is still allowed, and that battery optimisation is not closing the app. Our how-to guide has a full troubleshooting section.

Is Qadam available for iPhone?

Not yet. Qadam is currently an Android app. It is available as a direct download from this site and on the Amazon Appstore.

Can Qadam help me lose weight?

Walking is a proven part of weight management, and Qadam helps you stay consistent — which is the part most people struggle with. Combined with sensible eating, a daily step habit makes a real difference over weeks and months. Qadam is not a medical app, so please follow your doctor's advice for any health condition.
